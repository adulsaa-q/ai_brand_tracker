# Thailand Digital Consumer Behavior & Discovery Landscape (2026)
**Category Focus:** E-Commerce, Beauty Retail, and Multi-Channel Decision Journeys  
**Author:** AI Product & Market Intelligence Architect (Q)

---

## 1. Executive Summary
The Thai consumer purchasing journey has evolved from a linear funnel into an **omnichannel discovery loop**. Thai shoppers do not merely search on Google; they cross-verify recommendations across:
1. **Short-Form Video Discovery**: TikTok / Lemon8 / Instagram Reels (Visual demonstration & trend validation)
2. **Community Trust Verification**: Pantip, X (Twitter), Facebook Groups (Real-user reviews & authenticity check)
3. **Marketplace Comparison**: Shopee & Lazada (Price, flash sale vouchers, Double Day campaigns)
4. **Answer Engine / AI Search**: Google Search with AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, and ChatGPT for synthesized recommendations

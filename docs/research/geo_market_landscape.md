## 2. Competitive Landscape of GEO Tools (Global vs. Thailand Whitespace)

- **Global Tools (Profet, Peec, Otterly.ai, BrightEdge Generative Parser):**
  - High cost ($500 - $3,000 / month).
  - Weak support for Thai language nuances, Thai colloquialisms, and Thai local e-commerce platforms (Shopee, Lazada, Konvy).
  - Treat Thailand as a generic English-centric translation.

- **Thailand Market Whitespace (Our Strategic Edge):**
  1. **Native Thai Language Models:** Models that understand casual slang, code-switching, and local purchase intent.
  2. **Multi-Vertical E-Commerce Knowledge:** Real-time tracking of Thai retail giants, payment methods (PromptPay, COD), and Double Day campaigns.
  3. **Zero-Cost Sustainability:** Free-first local deployment on DuckDB + OpenRouter without recurring enterprise SaaS fees.
